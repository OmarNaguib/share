Buret: questions for the product team

31 October 2026

These are the open questions from our review of the design. Each one says what the design does today, what we need you to decide, and what we suggest.

If we don't hear back, we build our suggestion. You can still change it later. A few items say "Until you answer" instead: there, we build that until you decide.

Each item has a screenshot and a short video of the design at that spot. A red outline marks the place.

Questions are grouped by who should answer them. Inside each group, they follow the order we build in: sales and customers first, then stock and purchasing, then setup, then later work. Please answer the early ones first.

The small code next to each title (like PQ-14) is only there so you can point to it when you reply.

Words we use
Batch (تشغيلة):
boxes of one item that came in together, with one expiry date, one cost and one price.
Not for sale (ليس للبيع):
stock taken off sale but still in the pharmacy. Putting it there is called setting it aside.
Write-off (إعدام):
destroying not-for-sale stock, which removes it from stock.
Count (جرد):
staff count what is on the shelves and compare it with what Buret expects. Posting («ترحيل») closes the count.
Credit (آجل):
the customer pays later; the amount goes on their account, up to their credit limit.
Collection (تحصيل):
money received later, for a credit balance or a delivery order.
Drawer (الدرج):
the cash drawer of one till, handed over from shift to shift.
Contract (تعاقد):
an agreement with a company or insurer that pays part of its members' purchases.
Reorder list (كشكول النواقص):
the list of items the pharmacy needs to buy.
B-Connect:
the system the pharmacies use today. Buret replaces it.
First version, trial:
the first version is what the first pharmacies get. The trial is those first pharmacies using it.

Sales, returns, customers and the cash drawer

O-P1What each role may do, and its highest discount

What the design does todayThe permissions tab in the design is not final. The ready-made cashier setting there cannot sell, which looks wrong.

What we need you to decideWhat may each role do from the start? What is the highest discount each role may give?

Our suggestionUse the design's sample values: owner has no limit, manager 100%, pharmacist 10%, cashier 0%. The cashier can sell on the sales screen and use the drawer (here we follow the design's list of permissions, not its ready-made cashier setting). The owner can change any of this for each person.

No screenshot: the design has no screen for this.

PQ-3What must not be sold

What the design does todayThe design shows a «موقوف البيع» (stop-sale) badge on an item, but still lets you sell it.

What we need you to decideWhich stock must never be sold: only expired batches, or also stock close to expiry and items marked «موقوف البيع»? Can anyone ever override this?

Our suggestionAn expired batch is never sold, by anyone. No override. An item marked «موقوف البيع» can't be added to a new invoice; it shows as not for sale. If it is already on an open invoice, Buret flags it when the sale is finished. Stock close to expiry sells as usual, with the usual warning.

PQ-4Extra prices on the item card («الأسعار المتاحة»)

Also affects the item card.

What the design does todayThe item card has a «الأسعار المتاحة» list of extra prices, added with «إضافة سعر». These prices are not tied to a batch.

What we need you to decideWhat are these extra prices for? Who may choose one at the till? Should each price belong to a batch?

Our suggestionLeave them out of the first version. On the sales screen, the price list shows batch prices only. Choosing one is saved as a choice of batch. A refund always uses the price the customer actually paid.

PQ-2Rounding cash to 25 or 50 piasters

What the design does todayThe design shows exact amounts, down to the piaster.

What we need you to decideShould cash totals be rounded to the nearest 25 or 50 piasters («تقريب النقدية»)? If yes, the accountant must also say where the small difference is recorded.

Our suggestionNo rounding. Exact piasters, as in the design.

PQ-17Open invoices when the drawer is handed over or someone logs out

What the design does todayIf invoices are still open at drawer handover, at logout, or when the browser tab is closed, the design drops them without a word.

What we need you to decideShould Buret warn, keep the invoices, or stop the action?

Our suggestionOpen invoices stay saved for the person who made them. Handover and logout show a warning with a list of them. Deleting an invoice that has items asks for confirmation first.

PQ-16Printing a finished sale's receipt again

What the design does todayThe «فواتير البيع» page has no print button.

What we need you to decideMay staff print a receipt again? Who may do it? Should the reprint say it is a copy?

Our suggestionAdd «طباعة نسخة» in «فواتير البيع». The receipt prints marked «نسخة». Allowed for the owner, manager and pharmacist.

PQ-19When a customer can no longer buy on credit («آجل»)

What the design does todayThe design marks this rule as not decided yet.

What we need you to decideDoes credit stop only when the customer reaches their credit limit? Or also when a debt is overdue by more than a set number of days?

Our suggestionOnly at the credit limit. We can add a rule for overdue debt later.

PQ-29Showing the credit a customer has left, on the sales screen

What the design does todayThe design refuses a credit sale only at the very end, when the cashier finishes the sale.

What we need you to decideWhen the cashier picks credit as the payment, should Buret show how much credit the customer has left, before the sale is finished?

Our suggestionYes. Show the credit left, and whether the customer has overdue debt, in the credit payment.

PQ-22Giving the money back as a balance («رصيد للعميل»)

What the design does todayThe design's return screens offer no way to give the money back as a balance.

What we need you to decideWhen a cash, card or delivery sale is returned, can the money go to the customer as a balance to spend later?

Our suggestionNo balance refunds in the first version. The money goes back in cash or the way the customer paid. When a credit invoice is returned, the return first lowers what the customer owes (see D4).

PQ-23Points when items are returned

What the design does todayIn the design, a return does not touch the customer's points. Omar decided that Buret takes back the points earned on the returned items.

What we need you to decidePlease confirm this rule.

Our suggestionTake back the earned points in proportion to what was returned, rounded down.

PQ-25Points on credit («آجل») sales

What the design does todayIn the design, a credit sale earns points and lets the customer spend points right away.

What we need you to decideShould credit sales earn or use points?

Our suggestionNo. Credit sales don't earn or use points until you decide otherwise (Omar's choice).

PQ-24Changing how long points last

What the design does todayThe points rules have a setting «النقاط تنتهي بعد N يوم» (points end after N days).

What we need you to decideIf someone changes this number, does it affect points customers already earned?

Our suggestionNo. The new number applies only to points earned after the change.

PQ-26Order of discounts when there is a contract

What the design does todayWhen an invoice has a contract («تعاقد»), the design applies discounts in this order: first each line's discount, then the contract discount, then the invoice discount and the points discount.

What we need you to decideIs this order right?

Our suggestionKeep the design's order.

PQ-27Taking the contract from the customer

What the design does todayThe contract screen says a customer's contract applies by itself when you pick the customer. But on the sales screen, the cashier must pick the contract by hand. The two don't match.

What we need you to decideWhich one is right?

Our suggestionThe cashier picks the contract by hand. The customer's own contracts appear first in the list.

PQ-30Receipt for a credit payment («تحصيل»)

What the design does todayA collection («تحصيل») is money a customer pays toward what they owe. It is taken on the «تحصيل من …» screen in «الحسابات الآجلة».

What we need you to decideShould a collection print a receipt?

Our suggestionYes, with its own receipt number.

PQ-9Posting a count («ترحيل الجرد»)

What the design does todayIn the design, «ترحيل الجرد» only marks the count as posted. Stock does not change.

What we need you to decideShould posting change stock to match what was counted? If yes: when units are missing («عجز الجرد») or extra («زيادة الجرد»), which expiry date are they taken from or added to?

PQ-7A count finds an expiry date that has no batch

Until you answer, we buildOmar left this open, so there is no default yet. The row is saved, and stock does not change.

PQ-15After a write-off («إعدام»): tell someone, or wait for approval?

What the design does todayAfter a write-off, the design shows the message «تم إبلاغ المدير» (the manager was told). But nothing is sent. The notification bell also shows a sample item «إعدام بانتظار الموافقة» (write-off waiting for approval).

What we need you to decideShould a write-off notify someone, for example the owner? Or should it wait for approval before it takes effect?

PQ-14The «إعدام» tab on the «ليس للبيع» page

PQ-12Deleting an item

What the design does todayAfter an item is deleted, the design shows «تراجع» (undo) for 5 seconds. After that, the item is gone.

What we need you to decideAre 5 seconds enough? Or should deleted items stay visible and be restorable later? Who may delete items?

PQ-13Two batches with the same expiry date

PQ-32The words «العملاء المدينين» and «مدينين»

What the design does todayThe «الحسابات الآجلة» page uses «العملاء المدينين» and «مدينين». The design's own language rule keeps «مدين» for the accounting pages only (the journal and the trial balance).

What we need you to decideKeep these labels, or reword them?

Our suggestionKeep them as designed.

D3Editing a delivery order

The designThe lines of a registered delivery order are changed in place.

What Buret will doSame screens. But each change is saved as a new entry: a stock correction and a new version of the receipt. The sale becomes final at «تحصيل», when the money is collected.

D4Returning a credit («آجل») invoice

The designIt always offers a «نقدي» (cash) refund, even when the customer hasn't paid yet.

What Buret will doThe return first lowers what the customer owes. Cash goes back only for the part they already paid.

D5Discount limits

The designFixed limits of 20% per line and 10% per invoice apply to everyone, even the owner.

What Buret will doThe owner may go above them. They apply to everyone else.

D6Return period

The design«مدة الاسترجاع» is printed on the receipt but never checked. Anyone can change it on the returns page.

What Buret will doThe period is enforced. A late return needs a special permission and is recorded. The setting moves off the returns page.

D7Controlled drugs («صنف جدول»)

The designWhen a controlled drug is sold, the three check boxes are not saved, and the «بيع صنف جدول» permission is never checked.

What Buret will doThe three ticks are saved with the sale, and the permission is checked.

D8The cashier's menu

The designA cashier sees every page under «البيع».

What Buret will doThe menu shows only what the cashier may use. What a role can't use is hidden, not greyed out.

D9Deleting a customer

The design«حذف» removes the customer, without checking whether they owe money.

What Buret will doSame screen, but the customer is archived; their history and account are kept. Buret refuses while they owe money.

D10Who may change customer settings

The designEvery role can edit customers, credit limits, points rules and contracts.

What Buret will doEach of these has its own permission. Quickly adding a customer from the sales screen stays open to everyone.

D11Credit payments and the drawer

The designCash from a «تحصيل» never counts in the cash the drawer should hold.

What Buret will doIt counts, so collections don't show up as extra cash in the drawer.

D12Discounts bigger than the total

The designIf discounts add up to more than the invoice total, the design shows the total as 0.

What Buret will doBuret refuses the invoice.

D13Points on credit sales

The designPoints are earned and spent right away.

What Buret will doNo points until PQ-25 is answered.

D14Points on a return

The designPoints don't change.

What Buret will doPoints earned on the returned items are taken back (see PQ-23).

PQ-33VAT on purchases: before or after the invoice discount?

What the design does todayOn a purchase invoice, the design adds VAT («ض.ق.م») on each line first, then takes the invoice discount off the total. So «إجمالي ض.ق.م» (total VAT) does not change when there is a discount.

What we need you to decideIs that right, or should VAT be worked out after the discount?

Until you answer, we buildAs in the design: VAT before the discount. VAT is stored exactly on each line, and the invoice discount is spread across the lines.

PQ-35A bill total that doesn't match the sum of its lines

What the design does todayAt approval, the design warns when the printed bill total and the sum of the lines differ («الفرق: … — راجع السطور»), and still allows approving.

What we need you to decideWhen they differ, which amount does the pharmacy owe the supplier?

Our suggestionThe sum of the lines. Buret warns and allows approving, and the difference stays visible.

PQ-34The value of a return to a supplier

What the design does todayWhen the supplier's answer is recorded, the design fills «السعر» with the batch's purchase cost and lets you edit it. «قيمة المقبول» (accepted value) = accepted quantity × that price.

What we need you to decideShould the return be valued at the batch cost, or at the price the supplier accepts?

Our suggestion«السعر» starts at the batch cost and can be edited. The accepted value is taken off what the pharmacy owes the supplier.

PQ-45How long bill photos are kept

What the design does todayStaff can upload a photo of each supplier bill. The design says nothing about how long photos are kept.

What we need you to decideHow long must we keep them?

Our suggestionAs long as the invoice exists.

Already decided by Omar (for your information)

  • Moving stock to another store: while it is on its way it sits in a middle «في الطريق» (in transit) place. It cannot be sold from the sender, and the receiver gets it when they confirm receipt.
  • Moaz confirmed: the first version is for a single pharmacy. Several pharmacies (chain mode) come right after, as their own step.
  • Customer returns go back to stock, into the batch they were sold from.
  • Medicine prices come from the batch received and are never edited. Changing the price of a non-medicine item needs a permission.
  • The strip price is set once per batch: box price ÷ number of strips, rounded.
  • One payment method per invoice. No split payment.
  • Receipts print as an image, so Arabic letters join correctly.
