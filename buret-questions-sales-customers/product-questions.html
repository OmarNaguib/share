Buret: Sales and Customers questions for Moaz and Mostafa

9 November 2026

Omar sends this with the Sales + Customers founder build. It replaces the Sales part of the 2026-11-06 pack and adds Customers. Stock, purchasing, import and users come in their own pack.

The build

It is the Sales founder build with the customers module turned on. Choose «صيدلية تجريبية» on the first screen. It opens a demo pharmacy with a real item list (from a pilot pharmacy), a few invented items, four demo sign-in accounts (owner, manager, pharmacist, cashier) and four invented customers:

  • «حسام فتحي»: buys on credit, limit 1,500 ج, each invoice due 30 days after it.
  • «نادية إبراهيم»: holds 56 points from one earlier cash sale (invoice 1).
  • «شريف عادل»: linked to «شركة التأمين التجريبية» (local items 10%, imported 5%, the company pays 70%, medicines only).
  • «هالة مصطفى»: has a saved delivery address.

Nothing here blocks the build. Every item has a default that Buret follows today. Your answer either confirms the default or turns into a change.

When you try the build, note that item search forgives letter forms (أ/ا, ى/ي, ة/ه), diacritics and one typing mistake, but not two. S-22 explains the rules in full and asks how forgiving it should be.

Receipts don't print in this build: there is no screen yet to choose a printer, so a finished sale says «اختر طابعة الإيصال من الإعدادات». The printer card («الطابعات») comes with Settings. The receipt itself (58 or 80 mm, returns, «نسخة» reprints) is built.

For Moaz (M) · #368

QuestionWhen a seller asks for a discount above their limit and a manager approves it, can the manager approve any amount, up to 100%?

Options(1) an approval lifts every limit, and the audit record is the control; (2) an approval lifts the seller's limit only up to the approver's own limit and the pharmacy's caps; (3) as (2), and nobody can approve a discount on their own sale.

Buret today(1), calls A3-9 and A3-10. Self-approval is allowed: a manager can approve 100% on their own sale with their own password. Every approval is recorded with who approved it.

S-22How forgiving should item search be?

For Moaz (M)

QuestionA pharmacist types fast and often misses letters. How much should search forgive?

How search works in this build (in plain words)

  • These letters count as the same: أ إ آ ٱ and ا; ى ئ and ي; ؤ and و; ة and ه. Diacritics, tatweel («ـ») and invisible marks are ignored. «٣» and «3» are the same. Latin letters ignore upper and lower case.
  • Exact matches need the typed text in one piece, as it appears in the name: words typed in another order, or with a word skipped, find nothing.
  • One mistake is forgiven in the whole search (a letter missing, extra or changed, or two neighbouring letters swapped). This applies only to words of 3 letters or more, and also against the start of a word in the name.
  • Arabic does not find Latin or the other way round («بانادول» does not find «Panadol»). Other names for an item work only where someone entered them on the item.
  • At most 6 results. Order: a scanned box or batch label first, then barcode, then item code, then names that start with the text, then names that contain it, then other names, then matches with one mistake. Within each group, items that can be sold now come first, then alphabetical order.
  • Matches with a mistake are tried only when fewer than 6 exact matches are found, and never for a search of digits only.
  • Example: «بندول» does not find «بانادول» (two letters missing).

Options(1) also compare with the long vowels ا و ي left out, so «بندول» finds «بانادول»; (2) allow two mistakes on words of 6 letters or more; (3) leave it as it is and rely on other names entered on items.

Buret todayAs described above.

Why it mattersA search that finds nothing sends the seller to the shelf, or to sell the wrong item. A search that forgives too much fills the 6 places with wrong items.

Our recommendation(1). It costs little and catches most fast typing.

If you say noWe build (2) or leave it (3).
