Buret: Sales and Customers questions for Moaz and Mostafa

Draft, 2026-11-09. Omar sends this with the Sales + Customers founder build. It replaces the Sales part of the 2026-11-06 pack and adds Customers. Stock, purchasing, import and users come in their own pack.

The build. It is the Sales founder build with the customers module turned on. Choose «صيدلية تجريبية» on the first screen. It opens a demo pharmacy with a real item list (from a pilot pharmacy), a few invented items, four demo sign-in accounts (owner, manager, pharmacist, cashier) and four invented customers:

  • «حسام فتحي»: buys on credit, limit 1,500 ج, each invoice due 30 days after it.
  • «نادية إبراهيم»: holds 56 points from one earlier cash sale (invoice 1).
  • «شريف عادل»: linked to «شركة التأمين التجريبية» (local items 10%, imported 5%, the company pays 70%, medicines only).
  • «هالة مصطفى»: has a saved delivery address.

Nothing here blocks the build. Every item has a default that Buret follows today. Your answer either confirms the default or turns into a change.

When you try the build, note that item search forgives letter forms (أ/ا, ى/ي, ة/ه), diacritics and one typing mistake, but not two. S-22 explains the rules in full and asks how forgiving it should be.

Receipts don't print in this build: there is no screen yet to choose a printer, so a finished sale says «اختر طابعة الإيصال من الإعدادات». The printer card («الطابعات») comes with Settings. The receipt itself (58 or 80 mm, returns, «نسخة» reprints) is built.

How to read each item:

  • Question: the question in plain words.
  • Options: what you can choose between.
  • Buret today: what is built now, with the id of the call (so Omar can find it).
  • Why it matters: what it changes for a pharmacy.
  • If you say no: what we would change.

Arabic in «» is the exact text on screen. Where a question started as a GitHub issue, its number is given as (#nnn) so Omar can paste your answer there.

Who is asked: M = Moaz (product), D = Mostafa (design), M+D = both.

Omar can share screenshots of the screen items (design next to the app) on request.

1. Sales

S-1. A manager's discount approval: does it have its own limit? (M) (#368)

  • Question: when a seller asks for a discount above their limit and a manager approves it, can the manager approve any amount, up to 100%?
  • Options: (1) an approval lifts every limit, and the audit record is the control; (2) an approval lifts the seller's limit only up to the approver's own limit and the pharmacy's caps; (3) as (2), and nobody can approve a discount on their own sale.
  • Buret today: (1), calls A3-9 and A3-10. Self-approval is allowed: a manager can approve 100% on their own sale with their own password. Every approval is recorded with who approved it.
  • Why it matters: this is the main way money can leave the till as "discount" without a cash shortage showing. With (1), the audit record is the only check.
  • If you say no: say "cap approvals" for (2) or "cap approvals, no self-approval" for (3). The screens stay the same, with one new refusal message.

S-2. Empty "lost receipt" invoices after a restore (M) (#413)

  • Question: after a backup is restored, some receipt numbers were already printed but are no longer in the database. Buret fills each one with an empty, zero-total invoice so a receipt number never repeats and every gap is named. Should these show in the sales invoice list and in reports?
  • Options: shown (labelled as lost receipts); hidden by default with a filter to show them.
  • Buret today: shown, labelled as lost receipts, and left out of totals (they are zero anyway) (#277, #413).
  • Why it matters: a pharmacist scrolling the list will see invoices with no lines. Hiding them is tidier; showing them makes the gap visible to the owner and to a tax inspector.
  • If you say no: lists and reports hide them unless a filter asks for them. The invoices themselves stay (they are what stops a number being reused).

S-3. The business day for pharmacies that trade past midnight (M) (#321)

  • Question: a pilot pharmacy trades until about 02:30. Should the "day" end at midnight, or at a cutoff such as 04:00?
  • Options: (a) one setting for the whole app, default 04:00, used by reports, day totals and the return window; (b) keep midnight.
  • Buret today: (b) midnight. The drawer follows the shift, not the calendar date (call A4-9), so a shift that runs past midnight stays one shift.
  • Why it matters: with midnight, the evening shift's sales split across two report days, and "today's sales" at 01:00 shows almost nothing.
  • If you say no (choose a): a business-day cutoff setting is added, and reports, the return window and day totals use it. Omar must also sign this off.

The reason is one of «بيع قبل بدء العمل على بيوريت» / «فُقدت الفاتورة» / «سبب آخر». Only a user who may make returns after the return window can confirm it. The panel carries a note that its rules still need a pharmacy owner to confirm them. It is not in the design.
  • Why it matters: without it, the first weeks after the move create arguments at the counter, or returns done off the books. With it, cash leaves the drawer with no invoice behind it, so who may use it matters.

S-5. Opening a second box of the same batch (D) (#482)

  • Question: Omar ruled that each batch has at most one open box per store, so «علبة جديدة» is turned off while a box of that batch is open. How often do your pharmacists open a second box of the same batch on purpose?
  • Options: rarely (keep the rule); often (we change how open boxes are tracked).
  • Buret today: one open box per batch (Omar's ruling on #482, calls A7-2 and A7-6). While a batch has an open box, strips come from it first, and «علبة جديدة» is offered only for another batch. Known limit: loose strips that are returned until they add up to a full box count as a sealed box again.
  • Why it matters: the rule keeps the count of loose strips right without a number on every box. The cost is that the seller cannot open a second box of the same batch.

S-6. Closing an invoice tab that has lines (D) (#402)

S-22. How forgiving should item search be? (M)

  • Question: a pharmacist types fast and often misses letters. How much should search forgive?
  • How search works in this build (in plain words):
    • These letters count as the same: أ إ آ ٱ and ا; ى ئ and ي; ؤ and و; ة and ه. Diacritics, tatweel («ـ») and invisible marks are ignored. «٣» and «3» are the same. Latin letters ignore upper and lower case.
    • Exact matches need the typed text in one piece, as it appears in the name: words typed in another order, or with a word skipped, find nothing.
    • One mistake is forgiven in the whole search (a letter missing, extra or changed, or two neighbouring letters swapped). This applies only to words of 3 letters or more, and also against the start of a word in the name.
    • Arabic does not find Latin or the other way round («بانادول» does not find «Panadol»). Other names for an item work only where someone entered them on the item.
    • At most 6 results. Order: a scanned box or batch label first, then barcode, then item code, then names that start with the text, then names that contain it, then other names, then matches with one mistake. Within each group, items that can be sold now come first, then alphabetical order.
    • Matches with a mistake are tried only when fewer than 6 exact matches are found, and never for a search of digits only.
    • Example: «بندول» does not find «بانادول» (two letters missing).
  • Options: (1) also compare with the long vowels ا و ي left out, so «بندول» finds «بانادول»; (2) allow two mistakes on words of 6 letters or more; (3) leave it as it is and rely on other names entered on items.
  • Buret today: as described above.
  • Why it matters: a search that finds nothing sends the seller to the shelf, or to sell the wrong item. A search that forgives too much fills the 6 places with wrong items.
  • Our recommendation: (1). It costs little and catches most fast typing.
  • If you say no: we build (2) or leave it (3).
